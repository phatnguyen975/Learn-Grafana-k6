### Recommendation 1: Investigate Max Latency Outlier

**Basis:** `http_req_duration.max` = 922.86 ms indicates occasional severe queueing for a single or a few requests, likely due to SQLite write serialization locking during the exact moment of the highest burst. Source: `run/raw/summary.json → metrics.http_req_duration.values.max`

**Proposed action:** Investigate the backend logs for the request(s) that took ~922ms to verify if it was a lock contention issue in SQLite or network jitter during ramp-up. Consider implementing a slight retry delay or WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) tuning in SQLite if these outliers become frequent.

**Expected impact:** Reduction of the max outlier latency to be closer to the p95 range (< 50ms).

**Basis:** `http_req_duration p(95)` = 5.75 ms is substantially lower than the configured 500 ms threshold. The 500ms threshold was overly pessimistic about SQLite's performance at 100 VUs. Source: `run/raw/summary.json → metrics.http_req_duration.values["p(95)"]`

**Proposed action:** Lower the p95 SLO threshold for this endpoint in `tests/config/thresholds.js` and `perf-config.json` to a stricter value, such as 50 ms or 100 ms, for future tests.
